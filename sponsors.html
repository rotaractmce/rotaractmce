Sponsorship

Our sponsors play a crucial role in elevating the events conducted by Rotaract Club to new heights, making it a grand success. Their support not only enhances the quality of the event but also enables us to bring in cutting-edge innovations, top-tier exhibitors, and thrilling experiences for the audience. Through this collaboration, sponsors gain significant visibility, reaching a diverse and influential audience passionate about automobiles and technology. Their association with the Rotaract Club during events strengthens brand credibility, fosters valuable connections, and leaves a lasting impact on the community.

Our Valued Sponsors

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to Acharya Jewelers, Prestige, APJ PU College (Hassan), Bharath's PU Academy (Hassan), Kothari Builders, Nandagokula Convention Hall (Hassan), generous Rotarians from Rotary Club of Hassan Main, the super seniors of the club, and many more companies and individuals from Hassan and the surrounding community who have consistently supported our initiatives. Their contributions have been instrumental in making our events impactful and memorable.

Thank You, Sponsors!

We sincerely thank all our sponsors for their unwavering support and belief in the mission of Rotaract Club of MCE. Your generosity fuels our passion for community service and empowers us to create meaningful experiences that inspire and uplift. Together, we continue to make a difference.
